Social Preview Customize how your page looks when someone shares its link on supported social platforms.
💡 How to use this

Turn this on when you want to preview the title, description and image that people may see when your page link is shared. Turn it off if you do not want the social preview displayed inside the builder.

This is the title shown in a social sharing card. Example: Secure Account Verification — Fast & Simple
This is the short message displayed with your shared link. Explain the page clearly and tell visitors what they can expect.
Use a public image URL for the picture you want to appear when your page is shared.
Alternatively, choose an image from your device. After upload, the builder can use the uploaded image URL for the social preview.

Social Platforms These settings tell the builder which social accounts or sharing information you want associated with your page.
💡 How to use Social Platforms

You normally do not need to fill every field. Add only the social accounts that belong to your page or brand. For example, if your company has a Facebook page, enter its public page address. If you do not use a platform, leave its field empty.

Enter your public Facebook page address. Example: https://facebook.com/YourCompany
Enter your X/Twitter username, usually beginning with @. Example: @YourCompany
"Large Image" gives your shared link a large visual preview. "Summary" uses a smaller preview.
Enter your public LinkedIn company or profile page. Example: https://linkedin.com/company/your-company
